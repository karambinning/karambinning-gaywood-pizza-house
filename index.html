About the shop

Four decades on the same corner of Gaywood.

Gaywood Pizza House has been part of the Wootton Road parade for the best part of forty years — a small family takeaway that quietly fed a generation of King's Lynn teenagers, then their kids, and now their kids' kids.

There is no formula here. Pizza dough, a hot oven, a doner spit, a fryer, a chest-high counter, and the same telephone number people have been ringing since the late eighties. It's the kind of place you find by living nearby — not the kind of place that needs to shout.

If you've never tried us, the pizzas are stone-baked, the kebabs are sliced to order, and the kids' boxes will keep a six-year-old quiet for the length of a whole film. Ring through, or come and stand at the counter — both work.

What's on

The menu, in five honest sections.

No price list pinned up here — call the shop for what's current and what's in tonight. Below is what the kitchen does, day in, day out.

  1. 01

    Stone-baked Pizzas

    A classic short list — Margherita, Pepperoni, Ham & Mushroom, Hawaiian, the works. Plus sweet pizzas for anyone after pudding by the slice.

  2. 02

    Doner Kebabs

    Sliced to order off the rotator. Pitta, salad, chilli or garlic sauce — say how you want it when you ring.

  3. 03

    Burger Meal Deals

    Quarter-pounders, cheeseburgers, chicken burgers — done with fries and a can. The kind of order you can phone in and pick up on the way home.

  4. 04

    Pasta Meals

    A short pasta menu for when pizza isn't the answer. Filling, simple, served in proper takeaway tubs.

  5. 05

    Kids' Boxes

    Smaller portions, simple flavours, packed to travel. Built for the back seat of the car on the way home from football.
